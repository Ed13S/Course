Maths

Quadratic formula$x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$
Power rule$(x^n)'=nx^{n-1}$
Chain rule$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{du}\cdot\frac{du}{dx}$
Product rule$(uv)'=u'v+uv'$
Quotient rule$\left(\frac uv\right)'=\frac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}$
Integral of a power$\int x^n\,dx=\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+c$
Fundamental Theorem$\int_a^b f\,dx=F(b)-F(a)$
Binomial$P(X=k)=\binom nk p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$
z-score$z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$
Complex numbers$|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$,  $(r\,\mathrm{cis}\,\theta)^n=r^n\mathrm{cis}\,n\theta$
Dot product$a\cdot b=|a||b|\cos\theta$
Derivatives (radians)$(\sin x)'=\cos x,\ (\cos x)'=-\sin x,\ (\tan x)'=\sec^2x,\ (\sec x)'=\sec x\tan x,\ (\ln x)'=\frac1x$
Antiderivatives$\int\sin ax=-\frac{\cos ax}{a},\ \int\cos ax=\frac{\sin ax}{a},\ \int e^{ax}=\frac{e^{ax}}{a},\ \int\frac{f'}{f}=\ln|f|$
Trig powers$\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos2x}{2},\ \cos^2x=\frac{1+\cos2x}{2}$
Growth / decay$y'=ky\Rightarrow y=Ae^{kx}$
RC circuit$\tau=RC,\quad V=V_s(1-e^{-t/RC})$

Physics

Constant acceleration$v=u+at$,  $s=ut+\frac12at^2$,  $v^2=u^2+2as$
Circular motion$a=\frac{v^2}{r}$
Gravity$F=\frac{GMm}{r^2}$
Electric field$E=\frac Vd$
Magnetic orbit$r=\frac{mv}{qB}$
Flux and Faraday$\Phi=BA\cos\theta,\quad \varepsilon=-N\frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}$
Transformer$\frac{V_s}{V_p}=\frac{N_s}{N_p}$
Waves and double slit$v=f\lambda,\quad \Delta x=\frac{\lambda L}{d}$
Photons$E=hf,\quad KE_{max}=hf-\phi$
Relativity$\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}},\ t=\gamma t_0,\ L=\frac{L_0}{\gamma}$

🤖 Make my plan with the AI tutor

The tutor reads the starting-point tests done in this browser and builds a personal plan: a badge for every lesson, an order, a roadmap and notes. It needs your OpenRouter key in Settings. A free model cannot see handwriting, so it uses typed answers, "Not learned yet" ticks and timings.

The results and notes are sent to OpenRouter. A plan made this way replaces any plan already loaded.
